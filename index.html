About Us

Founded where the seafood export trade shaped everything we build.

Greenstar Packaging Company was founded in 2014 in Thoothukudi — a region shaped by its seafood export trade. That proximity shaped our specialization from day one: packaging that protects moisture-sensitive, high-value cargo through long-distance transit.

Watch the video above to hear the story directly from our team — how a semi-automatic plant in Valasamuthram grew into a specialist in seafood and dry flower export packaging.

"Protecting What Matters, From Thoothukudi to the World."

Vision

To be the packaging partner Thoothukudi's exporters rely on for durability, freshness protection and dependable delivery.

Mission

To manufacture packaging engineered for each client's product and export requirement, backed by consistent quality and on-time dispatch.

Commitment

Recyclable kraft board, responsible sourcing, and quality checks at every production stage — from raw material to pre-dispatch.

Corrugated Boxes

Fluted board · export & industrial grade

Our core product — multi-ply fluted board engineered for moisture resistance and stacking strength. Built for seafood export, dry flower shipments, industrial goods and heavy e-commerce transit.

  • Construction3-ply / 5-ply / 7-ply
  • Paper grade120–200 GSM Kraft
  • Load ratingStandard to Heavy Duty
  • FinishFlexo print, plain, or laminated
Best for: Seafood & frozen export, dry flowers, industrial goods, e-commerce

Duplex Boxes

Coated board · retail & branding grade

Smooth, printable duplex board for products that need to look as good as they're protected. Ideal for retail cartons, cosmetics, food packaging, pharma and gift boxes that carry your brand on the shelf.

  • Board typeGrey-back / White-back Duplex
  • Paper grade250–450 GSM
  • FinishOffset print, matte / gloss lamination
  • FabricationDie-cut, folder-gluer assembly
Best for: FMCG, retail, cosmetics, food, pharma, gifting
