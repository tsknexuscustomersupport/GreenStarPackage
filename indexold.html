Who We Are

Packaging engineered for the Indian supply chain

Green Star Packaging Company is a dedicated manufacturer of corrugated and duplex board boxes, built to protect goods through rough handling, long-haul freight, and everything in between. We don't sell generic cartons — every order is made to the size, strength, and finish a product actually needs.

Our production line runs under a certified quality management system, so the box that leaves our facility is consistent every single time — same dimensions, same strength, same clean finish, whether it's order one or order one thousand.

  • Two board systems — corrugated and duplex — covering shipping, industrial, and retail packaging needs
  • Any length, width, or height — built to your product, not a standard size chart
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management, audited and maintained
  • Direct manufacturer pricing — no middleman markup on bulk orders

Built on the corrugated flute.

The wavy medium sandwiched inside every corrugated sheet is what gives a box its strength — it's the same idea behind everything we build. Structure first, finish second, and nothing that doesn't earn its place in the wall of the box.

What We Manufacture

Two board systems. Every packaging need.

We currently manufacture two core product lines — each built on a different board system for a different job. Both are available in fully custom sizes.

Corrugated

Corrugated Boxes

Fluted board · industrial & shipping grade

Our workhorse product — multi-ply fluted board built for strength under stacking, vibration, and long transit. Used across warehousing, industrial component packing, export cargo, and heavy e-commerce shipping.

  • Construction3-ply / 5-ply / 7-ply
  • Paper grade120–200 GSM Kraft
  • Load ratingStandard to Heavy Duty
  • FinishFlexo print, plain, or laminated

Duplex

Duplex Boxes

Coated board · retail & branding grade

Smooth, printable duplex board for products that need to look as good as they're protected. Ideal for retail cartons, cosmetics, food packaging, pharma, and gift boxes that carry your brand on the shelf.

  • Board typeGrey-back / White-back Duplex
  • Paper grade250–450 GSM
  • FinishOffset print, matte / gloss lamination
  • FabricationDie-cut, folder-gluer assembly
