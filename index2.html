About Us

Founded where the seafood export trade shaped everything we build.

Greenstar Packaging Company was founded in 2014 in Thoothukudi — a region shaped by its seafood export trade. That proximity shaped our specialization from day one: packaging that protects moisture-sensitive, high-value cargo through long-distance transit without compromising on speed or cost.

Over the years, we expanded beyond seafood to serve the dry flower export trade — another category where product integrity depends entirely on the box. Today, our semi-automatic plant combines the consistency of mechanized production with the flexibility to handle custom orders across FMCG, food, textiles, pharma and e-commerce.

"Protecting What Matters, From Thoothukudi to the World."

Vision

To be the packaging partner Thoothukudi's exporters rely on for durability, freshness protection and dependable delivery.

Mission

To manufacture corrugated packaging engineered for each client's product and export requirement, backed by consistent quality and on-time dispatch.
